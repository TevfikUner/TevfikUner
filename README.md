## Featured Projects

### ✈️ [Turbulence Radar — AI Turbulence Prediction](https://github.com/TevfikUner/RadarFirst-V2)
*Graduation project*

An end-to-end system that estimates turbulence risk along real flight routes by matching OpenSky flight tracks with ERA5 weather data.

- Turbulence indices and an ML classifier trained on PIREP-matched samples
- FastAPI service with WebSocket live alerts, backed by PostgreSQL
- 3D route visualization with CesiumJS and MapLibre
- Wind-optimized route simulation (A*) with fuel and CO₂ estimates

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `scikit-learn` `CesiumJS` `Docker`

### 💈 [Salon Booking Platform](https://github.com/TevfikUner/Salon_Booking_App)
A full-stack barber & salon booking platform: one Flutter app for customers and businesses, backed by a FastAPI service.

- Concurrency-safe booking with row-level locks and idempotent writes
- Drag-and-drop staff calendar, campaigns, waitlist and monthly reports
- Load-tested up to 1,000 concurrent users; TR/EN localization; KVKK-ready

`Flutter` `Dart` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Firebase` `Docker`

### 🚗 Modif-AI — *Co-founder*
A startup that lets users preview vehicle modifications digitally, using image processing and 2D-to-3D transformation.

`Computer Vision` `Image Processing` `3D`
